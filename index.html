About Halcyon Estates

A small brokerage built around one rule. Treat your money like it is ours.

Halcyon Estates was started in 2014 by Marlene Vasquez after twelve years inside one of the big national brokerages. She wanted to build something smaller on purpose, a team where every client worked directly with an experienced agent instead of being handed off to a junior.

We do not chase volume. We chase referrals. That shows up in how we price listings, how we walk properties, and how we tell you to walk away from a house when it does not make sense, even when we lose a commission on it.

  • Local agents who actually live in the neighborhoods they cover
  • Honest pricing guidance backed by recent comparable sales
  • Same day replies on offers, inspections, and lender questions

What We Do

Real estate help for the moments that actually matter

We are a full service residential brokerage. That means we can sit with you through a first time buyer consultation, list your longtime family home, or run the numbers on a fourplex you are considering. Below is a quick overview of what each looks like.

Buying a Home

We sit down with you, learn what your week actually looks like, and only show you homes that fit the way you live. No pressure tactics, no rushing you into the first house that has good lighting.

How It Works

Three steps. No surprises in between.

We keep the process simple on purpose. You will always know which step you are on, what happens next, and who is handling it.

01

Find the Right Property

Tell us what matters most to you, the neighborhood feel, the school district, the morning commute. We pull listings that match and skip the ones that do not.

02

Make a Confident Offer

We pull recent comps, walk the property with you, and put together an offer that makes sense. You will know exactly why we are suggesting the number we suggest.

03

Close and Move In

Inspection, appraisal, lender coordination, title work. We stay on top of every deadline so closing day feels like a formality instead of a fire drill.
